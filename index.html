EXP-01 // FLAGSHIP ARTIFACT SHIPPED & LIVE

Null State: Terminal 01™

1–4 Players · 45–60 Mins · Ages 14+ · Tabletop Strategy System

A cybersecurity strategy card game built to simulate high-stakes netrunner exploits, volatile market crashes, and decentralized blockchain scoring without generic tropes. Program encrypted data fragments into 3 dedicated memory stacks, manipulate 6 fluctuating market tracks in real time, and execute rule-breaking Black, Grey, and White Hat exploits.

Dynamic Markets: 6 tracks (1–10) with real-time flash crashes
Memory Stacks: 3 active dedicated program slots
Hat Exploits: Black, Grey, and White Hat decks
Blockchain Ledger: SYSM end-game data scoring
[PHYSICAL MANIFEST]: 110 Data Cards (92 Script + 18 Glitch) · 24 Jumbo Location Sectors · 72 Market Cards · 6 Operator Dossiers · 6 Player Aids · Tabletop Gameboard · Custom D6 Scan Die · 48 Wooden Control Cubes
